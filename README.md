# The World in One Day — Sunday 22 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty `news-image-20261122` ("Sum up 22 November's
world news in one image").

This field drops the full-page many-items layout. Three big stories, big
and bold, and the rest only as short support — 11 stories across 6 regions,
every one of them from that Sunday:

- **COP31 collapses in Antalya**: nearly 200 nations, two days into
  overtime, and the draft Antalya Pact never passed — the final draft
  dropped even the fossil-fuel exit and the $120bn-a-year adaptation
  goal. First COP to end with no deal since Copenhagen 2009.
- **Russian warship sunk off Libya**: sea drones hit the corvette
  *Merkury* at Benghazi naval base at dawn; satellite images show her
  capsized, only the bow above water. Two drones hit, four were
  intercepted, 20+ more stopped that night, and a Maltese freighter was
  hit hours later. Who launched them: unconfirmed.
- **Candy factory fire in Cikande, Indonesia**: of 63 people in the
  area, 29 confirmed alive and 34 missing — 24 body bags already pulled
  from the ruins await DNA tests. Final toll not yet known.

Short items: Democrats lock the House at 258 seats (most since 1992);
a Russian drone flattens a five-storey block in Dymer, Ukraine;
Liverpool 4–0 Manchester United; Delta's system fault delays 1,600+
flights; Trump's warning to Iran's tankers; the Houthis' unverified
claim of a hit on Saudi Arabia's Rabigh refinery; drone strikes on
Khartoum; India's record 720/9 in Wellington. A UTC timeline, the day
in numbers, and a date-check box that names the earlier news
deliberately left out (ICJ Rohingya ruling, BepiColombo, the Las Vegas
Grand Prix) and what is still unproven.

Every story carries its source and status (CONFIRMED / REPORTED /
CLAIM) as reported on the day. All text is typeset by code; the page is
built to stay legible at thumbnail size: the date, the three big
stories and the big numbers first.

`main.py` publishes the bundled `entry_src.png` byte for byte; see
`VERIFY.md` for the double-run check.
